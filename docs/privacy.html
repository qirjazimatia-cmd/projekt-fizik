Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 22 November 2026 · Shqip · Support

BrainSpark is the science learning app of the Don Bosko school in Tirana, Albania. This page explains what data the app collects, why, where it is stored, who it is shared with and how you can delete it.

In short: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data. Data is used only to run the app for the school.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the Don Bosko school in Tirana (school office). Contact: email to be added before release.

2. What we collect and why

DataPurpose
Account: email, password (stored only hashed), role (student, parent, teacher), name, username, avatarSign-in, security and showing your name in class

Face ID is used only on your device to unlock your account; the app never receives face data.

3. Who can see the data

  • Teachers see the students in their classes and their work and results.
  • Parents linked with their child's code see published results and can read chats between teachers and their child.
  • Other students of the school see only username, name, avatar and XP (ranking, friends), and messages in chats they take part in.
  • The school office manages the student roster and activation codes.

4. Bosko AI and third-party services

Bosko AI is used only after you give consent in the app (you can withdraw it at any time). Before a question is sent, emails, phone numbers and similar details are removed automatically. Text in a photo is first read on the device; the photo itself is sent only when the text is not enough. Questions are not stored on our server, except answers you report as wrong.

5. How long we keep data

Account data is kept until the account is deleted. When you delete your account, the account and its related data (profile, progress, memberships, messages) are deleted. Files you uploaded (e.g. in projects or tests) are deleted by the school on request. Your row in the school roster stays with the school office but is no longer linked to any account. The security log keeps only the type and date of actions.

6. Children

The app is used by students, often under 18, as part of school. Student accounts are linked to the school roster, parents can link to their child, chats are filtered and every message can be reported or blocked. There are no ads and no marketing profiling.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data and object to its processing. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the web: Delete account. For other requests contact the school office. You may also complain to the Albanian Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, the date above is updated and important changes are announced in the app.
